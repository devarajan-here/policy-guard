# Company HR Policies (Sample Handbook)

## Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue 18 days of PTO per year, credited monthly. Unused PTO
up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year. PTO requests must be
submitted at least 5 working days in advance via the HR portal.

## Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Fully
remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed quarterly.

## Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses under ₹5,000 can be self-approved and submitted with a receipt
within 30 days. Expenses above ₹5,000 require manager pre-approval before booking.

## Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM–6:30 PM, Monday–Friday, with a flexible
1-hour window for start time. Core collaboration hours are 11 AM–4 PM.

## Code of Conduct
Employees are expected to treat colleagues with respect and professionalism.
Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind will not be tolerated and
should be reported to HR immediately via the confidential reporting line.

## Health Insurance Enrollment
New employees can enroll in health insurance within 30 days of their start date.
Open enrollment for existing employees occurs every January.

## IT & Data Security Policy
Company devices must have disk encryption and a screen lock enabled at all times.
Sensitive company or employee data must never be shared over personal email or
unapproved third-party tools.

## Confidentiality of Employee Records
Salary information, performance reviews, medical records, and disciplinary actions
are strictly confidential and are only accessible to HR and the employee's direct
manager on a need-to-know basis.
